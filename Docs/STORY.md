# LIST — STORY

> Consolidated from the story material already developed. Unconfirmed details remain explicitly marked.

## Story Identity

LIST is a first-person horror mystery built around curiosity. The story connects Julian Mori's present-day investigation with a concealed historical thread inspired by East Java around 1998.

The player should discover meaning through exploration, evidence, environmental details, and observation rather than through long exposition or constant cinematics.

## Core Story Premise

Julian Mori's parents, Nurhadi Mori and Maya Mori, are dead. The circumstances surrounding their deaths contain inconsistencies that become the trigger for Julian to investigate what really happened.

His investigation eventually connects his parents to a 1998 historical mystery and to **The LIST**, a record containing 21 names.

## Chapter 0 — Begin

### Established / Draft Core

1. Julian receives his father's camera.
2. The camera contains a recording connected to 1998.
3. Julian discovers a LIST-related document.
4. His own name appears on material originating from 1998.
5. A warning appears: **"Jangan biarkan dia melihat" / "DON'T LET HIM SEE."**
6. The first anomaly is introduced.
7. The concept of **SEEN** is introduced as a mystery element.

### Intended Ending Feeling

Chapter 0 should end with questions rather than complete answers.

The player should understand that Julian's parents were connected to something that predates him, while not yet understanding the full meaning of The LIST or the phenomenon.

## Chapter 1 — The First Name

**Status: EXPLORATORY / NOT FULLY CANONIZED**

The current exploration direction is for Julian to investigate the first name connected to The LIST.

A living witness may recognize the LIST and react as though it is something that should not be investigated.

Previously explored dialogue concepts include:

- **"Ini bukan daftar orang."**
- **"Jangan cari tahu."**

The horror remains observational rather than combat-focused.

An ending beat previously explored is that the next name belongs to someone Julian currently knows.

## Story Structure Principles

- Historical thread first; deeper personal meaning emerges gradually.
- The player should connect clues rather than receive complete explanations.
- Earlier details should gain new meaning after later revelations.
- Mystery should create curiosity, not merely confusion.
- Present-day investigation and the 1998 thread should eventually converge.
- Cinematics should be used sparingly so the game does not feel like a movie.

## Information Control

For each major revelation, track:

- World truth
- Public explanation
- Character knowledge
- Julian's knowledge
- Player knowledge
- False/incomplete information
