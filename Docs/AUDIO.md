# LIST — AUDIO DIRECTION

## Core Philosophy

Audio is a major source of presence and horror.

Avoid constant music or obvious scare cues. Silence and ordinary environmental sound should carry weight.

## Key Elements

- Environmental ambience
- Directional sound
- Foley
- Room tone
- Distant activity
- Subtle anomalies
- Intentional silence
- Imperfect recorded audio when tied to camera/media evidence

## Horror Audio

The player should sometimes hear something before understanding its source.

Audio can create uncertainty through:

- Sounds from apparently empty spaces
- Changes in room tone
- Distant movement
- Sounds that do not match the visible environment
- Recording artifacts
- Inconsistent or degraded source audio

## Music

Music should support atmosphere rather than constantly instruct the player when to feel afraid.
